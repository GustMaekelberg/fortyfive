Day one of the outbreak.
The highway is a trap.
Sam grabs his keys and heads for the highway.
So does everyone else.
Nothing moves.
In 2005, millions fled Houston before Hurricane Rita.
The jam stretched over 160 kilometres.
Cars ran dry in the heat.
Dozens died on the road, before the storm arrived.
Sam leaves the car.
Behind him, the horns fall silent.
Then something starts moving between the lanes.
He grabs a bike from a pickup.
Eighteen kilometres an hour beats gridlock.
By sunset, he finds a farm.
The taps are empty.
Sam knows a trick.
The basement water heater holds 150 litres of drinkable water.
On day two, something scratches at the door.
Just a dog.
Would you have stayed in the car?

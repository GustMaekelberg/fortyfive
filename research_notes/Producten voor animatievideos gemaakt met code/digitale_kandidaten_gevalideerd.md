## 1. Validation of the finance candidates (budget, debt payoff, savings, net worth/investment, FIRE, paid calculators)

### Takeaway
The finance spreadsheet niche is proven but low-ticket, and it is flooding with new 2026 listings. Named best-sellers earn roughly $5–$10 per sale on average:
- A debt snowball/avalanche sheet: about 7,105 sales and about $35.8k revenue, an average of about $5.04 per sale [TOOL].
- A wedding spreadsheet bundle: about $9.78 per sale [TOOL], covered in section 2.

Hosted finance tools sell at $9.99–$19 one-time (FireBlazer, a Payhip FIRE pack, Retirology). Only a subscription planner has disclosed big revenue: ProjectionLab at $1M ARR.

Buyer complaints are mostly about Excel compatibility breaking formulas, and about instructions or the effort the sheet demands. That points to a gap: a hosted web version, or a sheet with a video-led setup, that is "passive" rather than "meticulous".

### Inferences
- [SYN] Realistic price for a new finance sheet on Etsy is **$6–$12 list price**, discounted to about $4–$8 to compete. The FindNiche best-seller averages about $5. On an own store with video traffic, **$9–$15** is defensible for a sheet, and **$9.99–$19** for a hosted calculator unlock, matching FireBlazer, the Payhip FIRE pack and Retirology.
- [SYN] Many hits have listing IDs in the 4.5–4.6 billion range. I read this as listings created in 2026, which means heavy new supply in finance sheets. This is an inference from ID ordering, not a sourced fact.
- [SYN] Gaps a new product can credibly fill:
  1. **"Works in both Excel and Sheets" or a browser version**, so nothing breaks on export.
  2. **Low-effort setup**: three inputs and done, with a 60-second animated onboarding video.
  3. **A visual result moment**: debt-free date, a payoff race chart, envelopes filling. This is exactly what the reader's animation can show.
- [SYN] Seasonality is real. The Meghan Lim anecdote and RankHero's January peak (earlier notes) mean a December launch gets one January window. After that, sales depend on continuous content.

### Gaps
- No verified sales numbers for Gumroad or independent budget-sheet sellers.
- No revenue disclosed for FireBlazer, Retirology or FIRE Terminal.
- No paid rent-vs-buy or mortgage tool with disclosed sales was found; this was not searched further because of the search budget.

## 2. Widening the field beyond finance: visual products, demand and AI flattening

### Takeaway
Among non-finance visual products, the strongest hard evidence is for **event and life-admin spreadsheet systems**. A wedding planner bundle shows about 10,830 sales, about $105.9k revenue and 1,111 reviews [TOOL], with an average of about $9.78 per sale, almost double the debt-sheet best-seller.

US freelancer quarterly-tax trackers are a visibly growing 2026 sub-niche at $5–$25. Job-search trackers exist, but face many free Notion alternatives.

Static visual cheat sheets fall into two groups:
- **Very low-ticket and partly flattened by free or AI alternatives:** Excel shortcuts ($1–$12, with free WPS versions) and Spanish conjugation ($2–$5, bundles about $19, free PDFs).
- **Teacher-oriented shops with modest volume:** classroom posters, with 5–7k shop-level sales over 3–5 years.

Paid Anki decks, small paid web games and paid browser extensions show almost no money for newcomers. Curated data products show self-reported $2k/month and $3k–$7k outcomes, but they need a niche audience.

## 6. Regulatory and platform cautions (brief)

### Takeaway
- **Finance:** keep products generic and educational (calculators and trackers, no individual recommendations). The US publisher's exclusion rests on not giving tailored advice, and disclaimers alone do not exempt a seller. Stock and fund picks or model portfolios are the risky zone.
- **Tax trackers:** stale rules, such as the $600 1099 threshold, are a quality and liability risk.
- **Health and fitness:** not searched; avoid outcome claims.
- **Copyright:** summaries and cheat sheets must restate ideas in your own words. Selling a whole-book summary is the riskiest case. Anki decks need full ownership of text and images.
- **Etsy:** recipes and patterns have new AI rules; all downloads need a real preview; disclose AI.

### Cited Findings
- [Law-firm analyses] The publisher's exclusion rests on publishers not giving tailored advice (Lowe v. SEC test: bona fide, general, regular publication). Misleading content can still trigger the anti-fraud provisions. SEC staff no-action letters have excepted certain investment "calculators", and the SEC has been scrutinising analytics and model-portfolio tools. — [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/navigating-publishers-exclusion-under-advisers-act); [WSGR](https://www.wsgr.com/en/insights/informationor-advice-sec-regulation-of-information-providers-may-expand-to-include-providers-of-innovative-investment-analytics.html); [IBKR webinar PDF](https://brokerage.ibkr.com/webinars/spotlight-publisher-exclusion.pdf); [Kitces on financial coaches](https://www.kitces.com/blog/abcs-financial-coach-register-investment-adviser-status-sec-series-65-66-nasaa/); [Gordon Rees](https://www.grsm.com/resource/legal-compliance-for-financial-publishers-and-trading-educators/)
- [Etsy listing extract] The "$600 1099 threshold" feature should be checked against current IRS rules. — [Etsy 4514356720](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4514356720)
- [General sources, not legal advice] Fair use is case-by-case. Commercial use weighs against it, and market harm to the original is the key factor. Copyright protects expression, not ideas. — [Penn Online Books: fair use](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/fairuse-explain.html); [Authors Guild legal guide ch. 5](https://authorsguild.org/app/uploads/2022/11/Chapter-5_Writers-Legal-Guide.pdf); [MetaFilter thread on textbook summary](https://ask.metafilter.com/200601/Publishing-a-50-page-summary-of-a-textbook)

- Etsy 22 Oct 2026 AI rules: see section 4.

### Inferences
- [SYN] Excel shortcuts and verb tables are facts and are not copyrightable as such, but layout and wording are. Build your own design and do not copy SAP Press, Microsoft or WPS sheets.

# Format–product fit and monetisation models for faceless motion-graphics shorts (research notes, 19 Nov 2026)

Method note: page fetches were mostly blocked, so nearly all findings below come from web-search result extracts (one successful fetch: the HyperFrames GitHub README). Many numbers come from vendor blogs or self-reported founder figures. Each one is labelled. About 39 searches were used.

## 1. Format–product fit: which product types do animated or faceless videos sell, and which do they not sell?

### Takeaway
No independent 2025–2026 study compares animated/motion-graphics short-form video with filmed UGC by product category. The best evidence is old TV research (live action beats animation on recall in most categories), vendor ad-creative benchmarks (UGC stops the scroll better, while motion/studio video gets equal or slightly better click-through once people watch), and category sales data. That sales data shows TikTok Shop GMV is dominated by beauty, apparel and home goods, which rely on filmed demos and faces. Animation's comparative advantage is information-dense content (finance, productivity, software, data, education), where the product is itself information or an interface.

### Cited Findings
- Nielsen (2010, TV ads): across product categories, live-action ads beat animated ads. Brand recall was 22% higher for live action. The gap was smallest among viewers aged 13–35, and food was the exception, where animation did better. The study is old and covers TV, not short-form. — [Nielsen 2010](https://www.nielsen.com/insights/2010/should-marketers-re-think-animated-tv-ads-during-the-holidays); [TV Technology](https://www.tvtechnology.com/news/live-action-trumps-cartoons-in-ad-effectiveness)
- Ace Metrix (2013): animated ads underperform non-animated ads more among women than men, and the gap widens with age. — [Ace Metrix](https://www.acemetrix.com/?p=10702)
- Journal of Advertising Research (2021, Rx drug TV ads, experiment): animation had no effect on comprehension of risks and benefits or on behavioural intention, but produced "more negative attitudes toward the character, ad and product". — [ARF/JAR](https://thearf.org/access-knowledge-2/publications/journal-of-advertising-research/effects-of-animation-and-rotoscoping-in-direct-to-consumer-rx-tv-advertising-how-animation-vs-rotoscoping-vs-live-action-drive-perceptions-and-attitudes-toward-drugs/)
- Animated vs *static* display ads (Emodo, vendor): animated creative raised brand recall by 25% and ad recall by 17% over static. This is the relevant comparison for a static-image seller, but it is not a comparison with live action. — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media-buying/how-animated-creative-ad-formats-could-yield-stronger-attention-recall-metrics)
- Kantar (2025, Coca-Cola Christmas): an animal/CGI-character version outscored the people version on most metrics. Kantar attributes this to anthropomorphism and "more creative license when characters aren't realistic". This is one brand-ad case, not a format study. — [Research Live](https://www.research-live.com/article/news/effective-creative-principles-remain-the-same-whether-or-not-ads-use-ai-finds-kantar/id/5145503)
- DataAlly benchmark (vendor, 45 brands, Meta ads): median hook rate 21.5% for UGC vs 15.0% for studio/motion-graphics video (UGC 44% higher). Viewers who stay watch about equally long, and studio/motion video earns a slightly higher link CTR. — [DataAlly](https://www.dataally.ai/blog/ugc-ad-benchmarks)
- Stackmatix roundup (vendor): UGC-style Reels ads are claimed to give 35–45% lower CPA than polished Reels for direct response. It also notes UGC often has higher CTR but lower landing-page conversion, and says to judge on CPA/ROAS. The same piece notes that almost all stats in this category trace back to vendors' own case studies. — [Stackmatix](https://stackmatix.com/blog/ugc-style-ads-vs-polished-creative)
- Single-brand counterexample (Sans, Motion talk, Feb 2025): branded content beat UGC on CPA ($18 vs $29). — [Motion](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/how-to-analyze-meta-ad-performance-with-motion-sans-case-study/)
- Faces in ads: Realeyes (vendor, roughly 50,000 video ads) found human presence raises "creative efficiency" and encoding on average, but can hurt retention. JAR 2018 (print) found faces capture attention fast, but the advantage decays with exposure time. A widely repeated "38% more engagement with faces" figure has no traceable source. — [Realeyes](https://www.realeyesit.com/?p=7972); [Guido et al. 2018 JAR](https://ricerca.unich.it/bitstream/11564/711423/2/Guido_et_al._2018_JAR.pdf); [benly.ai (unsourced)](https://benly.ai/learn/ad-creative/face-detection-in-ads)
- TikTok Shop US 2025 GMV by category (third-party tracker Charm.io via AfterShip/FastMoss): Beauty & Personal Care was about $2.49B (22.5% of tracked GMV), womenswear 12.6%, followed by menswear, food & beverage and home. Total US TikTok Shop sales were about $15.8B (eMarketer). These are tracker estimates, not TikTok figures, and they conflict (Dashboardly claims $33.8B and a 28% beauty share). — [FastMoss](https://www.fastmoss.com/blog/u-s-tiktok-shop-2025-sales-recap-top-products-creator-trends-fastest-growing-categories/); [Beauty Independent](https://www.beautyindependent.com/tiktok-shop-nears-1b-beauty-sales-second-straight-quarter/); [Dashboardly](https://www.dashboardly.io/statistics/tiktok-shop-category-product-statistics)
- Oysterly Media (Australia survey, sample size not shown): short tutorial and demo videos are the strongest purchase trigger, and social media accounts for 29% of product discovery. — [eCommerceNews AU](https://ecommercenews.com.au/story/tutorial-videos-drive-australian-purchases-study-finds)
- Small 2025 survey (Romania, n=70): users engage most with entertaining, creative and educational content, and least with purely promotional product posts. — [univagora.ro](https://univagora.ro/jour/index.php/aijes/article/download/7338/2449/17736)
- Wyzowl (vendor; 2026 figures via secondary sites, which disagree): 96% of people have watched an explainer video to learn about a product (98% in 2025), and 85–89% say a video convinced them to buy. The claim that video on a landing page lifts conversion "up to 80%" is attributed variously to Wyzowl and Unbounce. These are self-reported survey answers, not measured conversion. — [Wyzowl](https://www.wyzowl.com/explainer-video-conversion-rate); [Levitate Media](https://levitatemedia.com/learn/video-conversion-statistics)

### Inferences
- Where the sale depends on tactile, sensory or identity proof (beauty texture, fit of clothing, taste, a gadget visibly working in someone's hand), filmed footage and faces have a structural advantage. These categories also dominate TikTok Shop GMV, so a motion-graphics-only seller would be competing on the platform's strongest turf with the weakest tool.
- Where the product is information, a number or a screen (budget spreadsheets, Notion/Sheets templates, calculators, apps/SaaS, courses, data newsletters, printable planners), a motion-graphics video can show the actual product (the UI, the chart, the before/after numbers) as faithfully as a camera can. These are the best-fit categories. The case studies in section 2 cluster here.
- Benchmarks suggest the weak point of motion graphics is the hook (the first 1–3 seconds), not the click once people are watching. Practically, the reader should invest most iteration in openings: a bold statistic, a provocative number, or a UI moment.

### Gaps
- No independent, 2025–2026, category-controlled study comparing organic animated shorts against filmed UGC on conversion. System1 has no published animation-vs-live-action study that I could find.
- The full Wyzowl 2026 report and the Oysterly category breakdown could not be accessed.

## 2. Real examples of faceless or graphics-led accounts that sell something

### Takeaway
The best-documented "graphics, not face" successes sell **information products** (courses, templates) in money, productivity and business-ideas niches. They built large audiences over years, mostly on Instagram and X, and their figures are self-reported. Their founders are known personalities even when the content shows no face. Evidence for anonymous, purely faceless motion-graphics accounts making money is almost entirely from course sellers and is unverifiable.

### Cited Findings
- **Visualize Value (Jack Butcher).** Minimalist black-and-white graphics, one per day since January 2019, spread organically on Instagram and X without ads. It sells courses (5 courses, 168 lessons). The first course was "How to Visualize Value", followed by "Build Once Sell Twice". Self-reported "$1M+ revenue in year one". Secondary sources say over $100K/month and $180K/month after 18 months, or $800K–$1M ARR. One account says 700 copies were pre-sold at $99 in 7 days. All figures are self-reported or come from podcasts, with no audit. — [visualizevalue.com/about](https://www.visualizevalue.com/about); [Scott D. Clary newsletter](https://newsletter.scottdclary.com/p/how-to-build-a-1m-year-course-in-1-5-years-with-jack-butcher-founder-of-visualize-value-9a0a43dec81b); [Callum McDonnell](https://callummcdonnell.substack.com/p/how-jack-butcher-built-visualise); [First MRR](https://first-mrr.com/study/visualize-value); [YesPress (promotional)](https://yespress.io/jack-butcher)
- **Personal Finance Club (Jeremy Schneider).** An Instagram finance account built largely on infographic-style posts (the founder also appears). Courses on index investing are priced around $79, with an average sale of about $60. He self-reported $100K in sales in the first 5 days after the 2020 launch (The Tilt says $110K in the first week). CNBC reports close to $1M in revenue since October 2020 (per his Instagram post) and 400K+ followers; a 2026 NGPF post says close to 700K. About 98.5% of revenue comes from two courses. Revenue later fell because a low-cost, lifetime-access course produces few repeat sales. The flagship course has 17,000+ students. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/select/this-self-made-millionaire-has-one-simple-rule-for-growing-wealth/); [PFC 2020 review](https://personalfinanceclub.com/2020-a-year-in-review-for-personal-finance-club/); [PFC how it makes money](https://personalfinanceclub.com/this-is-how-personal-finance-club-makes-money/); [The Tilt](https://www.thetilt.com/content-entrepreneur/jeremy-schneider-personal-finance); [NGPF](https://dev3.ngpf.org/blog/professional-development/5-things-to-know-about-conference-opening-speaker-jeremy-schneider/)
- **Easlo (Jason Chin), Notion templates.** Brand-led rather than face-led, built mainly on X. Insider verified $239,000 in one year with documentation. He reported more than $500K cumulative and posted in March 2023 that he was "halfway" to $1M. His following is about 314K–356K on X. Indie Hackers: "0 to $20k/mo and 356k followers by building Notion templates in public". — [Yahoo/Insider](https://money.yahoo.com/made-239-000-selling-budgeting-120626735.html); [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/YQNh2atLObz6Jj4SYeBl); [HelpKit case study](https://helpkit.so/blog/easlo-notion-template-business-case-study)
- **Thomas Frank (Notion templates).** He shows his face on a large YouTube channel, so he is not faceless, but he is a reference point for the template category. Reported at $100K–$175K/month, depending on the source. — [Kajabi](https://kajabi.com/blog/can-you-make-money-selling-notion-templates); [Built by Foundry](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/thomas-frank-notion-templates-2m-creator-business)
- **Visual Capitalist.** A data-visualisation publisher that uses animated mini-charts as a VC+ perk. Free site plus the VC+ subscription (under $6/month on a launch promotion; $300 lifetime offer in 2023), a poster store and a newsletter. No revenue disclosed. It is a company, not a solo creator. — [Visual Capitalist VC+](https://www.visualcapitalist.com/get-more-visual-capitalist-with-vc/); [lifetime offer](https://www.visualcapitalist.com/lifetime-vc-subscription-one-time-offer-expires-aug-24-2023/)
- **Small, realistic data point.** A Substack writer selling Notion templates since July 2024 had $0 for two months and about $250 by the end of September (self-reported). — [The Mindful Planner](https://themindfulplanner.substack.com/p/how-i-made-250-selling-notion-templates)
- **Faceless app marketing (TikTok slideshows/animations).** Couple Joy (relationship-question slideshows) reached 2M+ views, 60K+ likes and 200+ videos; downloads were not reported. DBLI is claimed to have reached about 40,000 downloads. A developer's automated slideshows got 100+ downloads. The "Rayz AI $80K MRR in 75 days" and "$120K/month slideshow hack" stories come from tool vendors (Shortimize, Stork) and are unverifiable. TripRank's rise was reportedly driven by networks of faceless affiliate accounts, which raises authenticity concerns. — [Playkit](https://playkit.substack.com/p/couple-joy-faceless-slideshow-growth); [Stormy.ai](https://stormy.ai/blog/tiktok-app-marketing-ugc-faceless-viral-hooks); [Postiz](https://postiz.com/blog/automate-tiktok-slideshow-marketing-ai-agents-postiz); [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/from-zero-to-80k-mrr-in-75-days-how-rayz-ai-mastered-tiktok-marketing); [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/articles/triprank-speed-tracker-tiktok-campaign-2026-01-19)
- **Guru claims (low reliability).** A podcast case study claims a faceless Instagram account reached 40K+ followers and $200K in 18 months; it promotes a course. A newsletter claims $125 in month 1, $410 in month 2 and $5,240 in month 6 with no face and no ads. Another creator's $40K month came from one viral reel after months of funnel-building, and their first $20K came from a course launch to a small audience, not from reels. — [Digital Empires podcast](https://chartable.com/podcasts/digital-empires/episodes/175945450-making-200000-from-a-faceless-instagram-account-case-study); [NamePick newsletter](https://namepick.beehiiv.com/p/how-i-built-a-5k-month-digital-product-business-with-zero-followers-2025-guide-250b); [Async](https://async.com/blog/monetize-faceless-instagram-reels/)
- **Critiques.** Critics argue that "YouTube automation isn't passive income, it's a content business". In many faceless-channel courses "the course about the channel was the real product". Red flags include upsells to high-ticket coaching. — [Side Hustle Review](https://sidehustlereview.beehiiv.com/p/the-truth-about-youtube-automation); [Skool AI Income Court](https://www.skool.com/ai-income-court-3070/faceless-ai-youtube-channels); [Adam Del Duca](https://adamdelduca.beehiiv.com/p/biggest-scam-new-youtubers-fall)
- A 90-day faceless Instagram experiment described the first 30 days as "slow and frustrating". A faceless Shorts experiment got 80K views and 372 subscribers in month 1, with no sales reported. — [Medium](https://medium.com/@vireuoess/i-ran-a-faceless-instagram-page-for-90-days-d6b6ffc93b24); [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-invested-5-min-per-day-for-a-month-into-my-faceless-youtube-channel-results-f919afdc1e)

### Inferences
- The success cases share these traits: (a) information niches (money, productivity, business); (b) the creator *is* the product's author (courses, templates), so margins are close to 100%; (c) years of daily posting before the big numbers; (d) a strong graphic signature (consistent style) instead of a face. That fits motion graphics, which can be the "signature".
- All the large cases suffer from survivor bias, and none was built on pure motion-graphics shorts on TikTok/Reels in 2025–2026. The reader should treat them as upper-bound outcomes after multi-year effort, not as targets for a €500 experiment.

### Gaps
- No verified example of an anonymous, purely animated short-form account with disclosed revenue in 2025–2026.
- No documented failure studies with numbers (beyond anecdotes). Reddit threads could not be fetched.

## 3. Monetisation models for a small faceless account (economics, Belgium eligibility, cost)

### Takeaway
For a Belgian resident, platform payouts are largely closed or negligible at small scale. TikTok Creator Rewards is not available in Belgium per third-party lists. YouTube Shorts revenue share is available in Belgium, but from 1 February 2027 it requires 10M qualified Shorts views per rolling 90 days. Facebook's eligibility is unconfirmed. The best learning per euro is selling your **own low-ticket digital product** (template, spreadsheet, mini-course) or **SaaS affiliate** links, both startable for well under €100. TikTok Shop affiliate is available in Belgium since June 2026, but suits physical products, which is the weaker category for animation.

### Cited Findings
**Platform payouts**
- TikTok Creator Rewards: third-party lists (mid-2026) name about 8 countries (US, UK, DE, FR, JP, KR, BR, MX; some lists swap in CA/IT). None includes Belgium, and the account must be registered in an eligible country. Belgium is eligible only for *Effect* Creator Rewards (paid for AR effects; added March 2024). Confirm in TikTok Studio. — [ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-fund-countries/); [TechCrunch 2024 (Effect rewards)](https://techcrunch.com/2024/03/12/tiktok-letting-creators-more-countries-get-rewarded-effects/amp/); [Comparitech](https://comparitech.com/blog/vpn-privacy/access-tiktok-creator-program-in-another-country)
- TikTok Creator Rewards pays only for original videos over 1 minute. RPM is estimated at about $0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views, with US up to about $2 (vendor estimates; US about $0.85, UK $0.70, DE $0.62). One creator reportedly earned $123 on 16M views because few views qualified. — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/how-much-does-tiktok-pay/); [ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-rewards-program/); [elev8or](https://www.elev8or.io/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-rpm-2026)
- YouTube Partner Program: Belgium is on Google's list of countries for the expanded YPP. Shorts revenue share gives creators 45% (long-form 55%). — [YouTube Help 13429240](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240); [YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/new-opportunities-to-earn-and-changes-to-the-youtube-partner-program/)
- YouTube change announced 10 August 2026, effective 1 February 2027: channels need 10M qualified Shorts views in a rolling 90-day window to keep receiving Shorts Creator Pool revenue. The entry threshold for new applicants doubles to 20M Shorts views in 90 days, or 8,000 watch hours in 365 days, plus 1,000 subscribers. Cartoon Brew: the rule "will push animators away from Shorts". — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/); [NewscastStudio](https://www.newscaststudio.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-monetization-changes-2027/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/417147/youtube-doubles-eligibility-requirements-for-creat.html); [Cartoon Brew](https://cartoonbrew.com/shorts/youtube-shorts-monetization-rule-changes-265682.html)
- Facebook/Meta: there is no public country list for Facebook Content Monetization. Belgium was included in in-stream ads (2023) and appears for Stars and Subscriptions. Treat as likely but unconfirmed. Check the Professional Dashboard. — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/?p=153161); [Fortune 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/03/14/facebook-creators-monetization/); [Facebook fan subs](https://creators.facebook.com/tools/fan-subs)

**Affiliate**
- TikTok Shop launched in Belgium (with AT, NL, PL) on 15 June 2026. Approved EU creators can earn affiliate commissions. Seller fee is about 9% (sources differ on base and timing). TikTok Shop now operates in 10 European countries. — [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/tiktok-expands-tiktok-shop-to-austria-belgium-the-netherlands-and-poland-starting-june-15/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-shop-grows-to-new-eu-merchant-countries/821443/); [Clickforest](https://www.clickforest.com/en/blog/tiktok-shop-belgium); [Metricool NL](https://metricool.com/nl/tiktok-shop-nederland-belgie/)
- Amazon Associates (US): non-US associates must complete a tax interview (W-8BEN). Amazon withholds up to 30% unless a valid treaty claim is made. The Belgium treaty rate was not found. Commission rates (third-party reading of Amazon's schedule): Amazon Games 20%, Luxury Beauty 10%, "All other categories" 4%, electronics/computers/home improvement 1–4.5%, health about 1%. Furniture/home/garden were cut to 3% by 2025. — [Amazon tax interview help](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GPFZ6W6CF4E5BD9V); [Amazon withholding help](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GCZPLBSR44H9SS7J); [Amazon commission statement](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ/); [Lasso](https://getlasso.co/amazon-affiliate-commission-rate/); [Flywheel](https://flywheeldigital.com/blog/amazon-associates-program-updates)
- SaaS affiliate: typically 20–30% recurring. One vendor blog cites an "average 25% recurring for 12 months". Canva pays a flat fee of up to $36 per referral. Notion's terms are inconsistently reported (50% of first payment, or 50% for 12 months, or "TBD 2026"). — [Supademo](https://supademo.com/blog/saas-affiliate-programs); [Rewardful directory](https://www.rewardful.com/saas-affiliate-programs); [Refgrow](https://refgrow.com/blog/saas-affiliate-program-examples)

**Newsletter sponsorship**
- beehiiv Ad Network pays CPM per 1,000 unique opens (example: $10 CPM × 10,000 opens = $100) or CPC. It requires a paid beehiiv plan, and payouts go through Stripe Express (Belgium is supported by Stripe; regional restrictions exist). beehiiv's guide: new newsletters get $50–$250 per placement. Example: 3,000 subscribers at $25 CPM = $75 per sponsorship. — [beehiiv Ad Network FAQ](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/17507491038231-Ad-Network-FAQ); [beehiiv sponsorship cost](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/newsletter-sponsorship-cost)

**Own digital product**
- Gumroad (third-party dataset by InsightRaider, 146,271 products): median creator earns about $72/month, fewer than 5% earn over $1,000/month, 44% of products have $0 in revenue, and revenue is extremely concentrated in the top 1% (77–99.5% depending on dataset version). This is not Gumroad's own data. — [InsightRaider / DEV](https://dev.to/insightraider/72month-thats-what-the-median-gumroad-creator-earns-these-5-niches-break-the-pattern-49f0); [InsightRaider dataset](https://insightraider.com/en/data/gumroad-statistics-2026)
- Kit (ConvertKit) creator-economy reports: digital products were the top planned new income stream (2023). Digital products and services were the top two income streams for creators earning over $50K and over $150K (2022). 76% of surveyed creators earned some income (2023). — [Kit 2023 report](https://convertkit.com/reports/creator-economy-2023); [Creatorbread on 2022 report](https://creatorbread.com/blog/top-takeaways-from-convertkit-2022-state-of-the-creator-economy-report); [Kit 2024 report](https://convertkit.com/reports/creator-economy-2024)
- An established faceless account's estimated income mix puts digital products at 20–40% (blog estimate). — [BizToolkit](https://www.biztoolkit.co/post/how-much-do-faceless-instagram-accounts-make-in-2026)

### Inferences
- **Cost under €500.** Own digital product: about €0–€50 (Gumroad/Payhip/Lemon Squeezy take a percentage, there is no monthly fee, plus a domain if you want one). SaaS/Amazon affiliate: €0. Newsletter: €0 on free tiers, though beehiiv's Ad Network needs a paid plan. Physical product: inventory plus samples quickly consume €200–€400, and it is the poorer format fit. Platform payouts: €0 cost but realistically €0 revenue for a Belgian at small scale.
- **Learning per euro.** An own low-ticket digital product ($9–$29) gives the fastest full-funnel feedback (views → profile → click → checkout) and full margin. SaaS affiliate is the cheapest test of "can my animations make someone click and buy", and its UI-heavy products are a natural fit for motion graphics. Amazon affiliate at 1–4% commission on gadgets is a poor fit (low margin, physical products, and filmed-demo competition).
- YouTube's 2027 change makes Shorts revenue share effectively irrelevant for a new account: 10M views per 90 days is far beyond a beginner.
- Belgian tax and VAT (EU VAT on digital products sold to EU consumers via OSS; Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy act as merchant of record for VAT) is relevant but was not researched here.

### Gaps
- An official TikTok list confirming Belgium is excluded from Creator Rewards (only third-party lists were found).
- The Belgium–US treaty rate for Amazon commissions.
- Confirmation of Facebook Content Monetization eligibility for Belgium.
- Whether an *own-account* TikTok Shop affiliate creator in Belgium can promote to US audiences (likely not: TikTok Shop is market-bound, so a Belgian account would promote EU shop products to an EU audience).

## 4. Realistic funnel benchmarks for organic short-form video (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
Expect a median of roughly 300–750 views per Reel for small Instagram accounts and about 500 views per TikTok post regardless of posting frequency. Results are extremely skewed: a few outliers bring most reach. Link-in-bio clicks are typically 1–3% of profile visits (up to about 5–10% for top accounts), and low-ticket product pages convert at roughly 3–6% of visitors (weak data). Combined, 10,000 views typically yields single-digit sales, often zero.

### Cited Findings
- Socialinsider 2025 benchmark (data January 2023 to December 2024): Reels from small accounts average about 300 views. Accounts over 100K followers average about 15K per Reel. — [Socialinsider benchmarks PDF](https://www.socialinsider.io/data-geeks/instagram_benchmarks_2025.pdf)
- Socialinsider (July 2025 blog): Reels average 470 views for accounts with 1K–5K followers and 750 for 5K–10K. Carousels outperform Reels for small accounts on several metrics. — [Socialinsider views per format](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-views/)
- Colorlib, citing Socialinsider: Reels under 5K followers average 625 views; 100K–1M followers average 18,300. — [Colorlib](https://colorlib.com/wp/instagram-engagement-rate/)
- Buffer (October 2025, 11.4M TikTok posts, more than 150K accounts, fixed-effects regressions): compared with posting once a week, 2–5 posts/week gives up to 17% more views per post, 6–10 gives up to 29%, and 11+ gives up to 34%. **Median views per post hover around 500 regardless of frequency.** Higher frequency mainly makes the distribution more skewed, raising the chance of an outlier. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-post-frequency-improves-per-post-reach-report-buffer/802443/)
- Link-in-bio CTR (vendor blogs): 1–3% of profile visitors click (Hopp, TT Calculator); 2–5% for most niches and 10%+ for top creators (Unilink); Instagram bio-link about 2–4%, top accounts 8–10%. Linktree's "65%" figure is clicks per Linktree page session (Oct 2025 to Mar 2026), a different denominator. The first three links get 130% higher CTR than the bottom seven (Linktree 2023). — [Hopp](https://www.hopp.co/post/understanding-ctr-and-clicks-on-your-link-in-bio); [TT Calculator](https://ttcalculator.net/calculators/conversion-rate/tiktok-conversion-rate-optimization/); [Unilink](https://www.unilink.us/blog/link-in-bio-analytics-track-audience); [Linktree](https://linktr.ee/blog/your-bio-link-is-the-internets-busiest-intersection); [Linktree 2023 creator report](https://linktr.ee/blog/linktree-2023-creator-report)
- Product-page conversion: one anecdotal 30-day test on the same Instagram traffic gave Stan Store 4.8% vs Gumroad 3.1% (no method or sample size). Generic landing pages: Unbounce 2024 all-industry median 6.6%; a HubSpot-cited average of 5.89%. — [ecomm.design](https://ecomm.design/stan-store-vs-gumroad/); [Thinkific](https://www.thinkific.com/blog/landing-page-conversion-rate/); [TrueProfit](https://trueprofit.io/blog/what-is-a-good-conversion-rate)
- Sites with video convert at 4.8% vs 2.9% without (Wishpond, via secondary source; general e-commerce). — [Levitate Media](https://levitatemedia.com/learn/video-conversion-statistics)
- TikTok bio link: personal accounts reportedly need 1,000 followers for a clickable website link; registered or verified business accounts can add one earlier. Availability varies by market (third-party guides). — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/how-to-put-a-link-in-tiktok-bio/); [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/blog/how-to-add-link-to-tiktok-bio); [Stan](https://stan.store/blog/tiktok-link-bio-requirements-2026-guide/)

### Inferences
- **Worked example (illustrative, combining the ranges above).** 30 posts × about 500 median views = 15,000 views. Profile-visit rate from a Reel is not in the sources; assuming 1–2% gives 150–300 profile visits. At a 1–3% link CTR that is 2–9 store visits. At about 3–5% conversion that is 0–1 sales. **Without an outlier, a month of organic posting often yields zero sales.** One 200K-view outlier changes this to roughly 1–6 sales. This is why the profitable cases took many months, and why the first experiment should be designed around learning (hook rates, profile-visit rates) rather than revenue.
- On TikTok, a new personal account without 1,000 followers may have no clickable link at all. Instagram (bio link from day one) or a business account is preferable for a link-driven funnel.
- Posting frequency of 3–5 per week captures most of Buffer's measured benefit. Programmatic production makes higher volume cheap, but the median does not move.

### Gaps
- No reliable public benchmark for view-to-profile-visit rate or follower-to-customer rate. The figures found were unsourced blog claims and are excluded here.
- No hard data on how many posts it takes to reach "consistent reach". Practitioner accounts say "months".
- Socialinsider figures are averages, not medians. The Buffer median is TikTok-wide, not new-account-specific.

## 5. Platform rules and risks (labelling, originality, mass-production policies)

### Takeaway
Programmatic motion graphics (animated text, charts, UI mock-ups) are **not** "realistic synthetic media" and do not need AI labels on YouTube or TikTok under the stated policies. Meta's equivalent rule also targets photorealistic content (not verified in this round). The real risk is the **originality and mass-production** policies: YouTube's July 2025 "inauthentic content" rename explicitly names template-made videos with minimal variation and slideshows without meaningful narration as non-monetisable. Meta (July 2025) and Instagram penalise unoriginal reposting and aggregation. A templated HyperFrames pipeline that churns near-identical videos sits close to these lines.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube, effective 15 July 2025: "repetitious content" was renamed "inauthentic content". It was described as a clarification (Rene Ritchie: "minor update"). Examples cited: videos made from a single template with minimal differences, and slideshows lacking meaningful narration, commentary or educational value. Violations can remove monetisation from the **entire channel**. Reaction and clips channels are not specifically targeted. AI use is acceptable; mass-produced spam is not. There is no exhaustive definition. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-targets-mass-produced-content-in-monetization-update/550337/); [SEJ clarification](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-monetization-update-spam-not-reaction-channels/550755/); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/youtube-clarifies-monetisation-policy-targeting-inauthentic-content-9493155); [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/youtube-clarifies-inauthentic-content-policy-changes/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/youtube-updates-monetisation-policies-ai-and-repetitive-content-ban-begins-july-15-1.500192660)
- YouTube altered or synthetic content disclosure: required only for realistic content a viewer could mistake for a real person, place or event. Clearly unrealistic content such as **animation**, special effects and minor edits is exempt. Mandatory enforcement reportedly began 21 May 2025 (AIR). YouTube may apply labels itself. — [YouTube Help 14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491); [Google blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/how-were-helping-creators-disclose-altered-or-synthetic-content/); [AIR 2026 guide](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/youtube-ai-disclosure-in-2026-label-it-yourself-or-youtube-will); [Android Central](https://androidcentral.com/apps-software/youtube-ai-synthetic-content-disclosure)
- TikTok: labels are required on AI content with realistic images, audio or video. Cartoon-style AI falls outside the rule (Fortune, 2024). Generic text-to-speech voiceovers and AI-written scripts reportedly do not require a label (secondary). TikTok auto-labels via C2PA Content Credentials metadata, and creator-applied labels cannot be removed. Updated Community Guidelines took effect 13 September 2025. Ads have separate disclosure rules. — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2024/05/09/tiktok-ai-generated-content-label-misinformation/); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/tiktok-new-label-ai-generated-content-432127); [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/ai-disclosure-policy-for-tiktok/); [Magic Hour (secondary)](https://magichour.ai/blog/tiktok-ai-generated-content-labels); [WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/tiktok-overhauls-guidelines-ai-labels-misinfo-bans-start-sept-2025)
- TikTok Creator Rewards pays only for "original" videos over 1 minute (repeated from section 3). — [ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-rewards-program/)
- Meta, July 2025: Facebook penalises accounts that repeatedly repost others' content without permission or "meaningful enhancements". Penalties are loss of monetisation and reduced distribution. Post-level insights in the Professional Dashboard flag unoriginal content. Meta acted against about 500,000 spam accounts in H1 2025. Critics report wrongful automated flags. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/14/following-youtube-meta-announces-crackdown-on-unoriginal-facebook-content); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/meta-says-its-cracking-down-on-facebook-creators-who-steal-content-203713569.html?src=rss); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/meta-follows-youtube-in-crackdown-on-unoriginal-content/551096/)
- Instagram: since 2022, ranking favours original content over aggregators, using classifiers that estimate originality probabilistically. In 2024 Mosseri said reposts would be swapped for originals in recommendations and serial aggregators removed from recommendations. — [TechCrunch 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/20/instagram-ranking-system-recommend-original-content/?tpcc=tcplustwitter); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html); [Tubefilter 2024](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/04/30/instagram-meta-threads-original-content-algorithm-bonus-program-monetization/amp/)
- YouTube's February 2027 Shorts rule (10M views per 90 days) is expected to push animators away from Shorts. — [Cartoon Brew](https://cartoonbrew.com/shorts/youtube-shorts-monetization-rule-changes-265682.html)

### Inferences
- Self-made programmatic animation is "original" content as long as each video carries distinct substance (new data, a new argument, a new example) and ideally the creator's own voiceover or commentary. Re-rendering one template with swapped numbers, or reusing a single TTS voice and structure across hundreds of videos, matches YouTube's named examples of inauthentic content.
- The originality policies mostly bite *platform monetisation*. A seller who monetises via their own product or affiliate links is less exposed, but reduced distribution (Meta, Instagram) still hurts.
- AI labels: not needed for charts, text or UI animation. If AI-generated photorealistic imagery or realistic voice clones are ever added, labelling becomes relevant. C2PA metadata from AI image tools can trigger TikTok's auto-label.

### Gaps
- Meta's AI-label ("AI info") policy text for non-realistic graphics was not verified in this round.
- No official documentation was found on TikTok "originality" scoring for templated content.
- No measured data on how templated or faceless channels were affected after July 2025 (only anecdotes).

## 6. What makes a product "explainable" in a 15–40 second motion-graphics video

### Takeaway
The research turned up no formal study. The evidence points the same way, though. Animation matches live action on comprehension (JAR 2021), wins over static images, and loses on emotional and sensory proof (Nielsen 2010). The success cases sell products whose value is a number, a system or a screen. So a product is "explainable" when its benefit can be **shown as data or an interface**, not felt or touched.

### Cited Findings
- Animation did not reduce comprehension of risks and benefits versus live action, but produced more negative attitudes toward the product (JAR 2021). — [ARF/JAR](https://thearf.org/access-knowledge-2/publications/journal-of-advertising-research/effects-of-animation-and-rotoscoping-in-direct-to-consumer-rx-tv-advertising-how-animation-vs-rotoscoping-vs-live-action-drive-perceptions-and-attitudes-toward-drugs/)
- Social proof is the single highest-impact element on a digital-product landing page because buyers cannot inspect the product (SamCart, vendor). — [SamCart](https://www.samcart.com/blog/landing-page-optimization-12-data-backed-strategies-that-double-conversions)
- Visualize Value distils one idea per image. Personal Finance Club sells index-investing courses with numeric arguments (compound growth). Easlo sells budgeting and productivity Notion systems; the Insider story is specifically about budgeting templates. — [Visualize Value](https://www.visualizevalue.com/about); [PFC](https://personalfinanceclub.com/this-is-how-personal-finance-club-makes-money/); [Yahoo/Insider](https://money.yahoo.com/made-239-000-selling-budgeting-120626735.html)
- HyperFrames' own suggested use cases include data visualisations, product launches, docs/PDF-to-video explainers and social videos with captions. Its registry has about 450 blocks, including charts, maps and titles. — [HyperFrames GitHub](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes)

### Inferences (checklist derived from the above; not from a study)
- **Quantifiable benefit**: "save 5 hours/week", "€12,000 more at 65". Animated counters and bar charts can carry this.
- **Before/after expressible as numbers or screens**: a messy spreadsheet becoming a clean dashboard; a budget going from red to green.
- **Visual interface is the product**: templates, apps, SaaS, calculators. A UI mock-up animation *is* a product demo.
- **Clear mechanism in 3 steps or fewer**: "track → categorise → see where money goes".
- **A hook statistic that is true and surprising**: important because motion graphics' weak point is the hook rate (section 1).
- **Low price and low risk** ($5–$30 digital): it lowers the trust barrier that a missing face creates.
- **Poor fits**: products whose value is sensory (texture, taste, fit, scent), whose trust depends on a person (health or safety claims, coaching), or which need proof of physical functioning (gadgets). This matches the earlier project finding on night-driving glasses.
- **Examples of good fits**: personal-finance spreadsheets and calculators; Notion/Sheets planners for freelancers or students; an app or SaaS via affiliate links (show the UI flow); data-driven newsletters ("one chart a day"); printable or digital study systems; "how X works" explainers leading to a paid guide.

### Gaps
- No empirical study measuring which product attributes predict conversion from animated short-form specifically.

## 7. HyperFrames: what it is (verified)

### Takeaway
HyperFrames is HeyGen's open-source (Apache-2.0) "video as code" framework. You write HTML/CSS plus seekable JS animations (GSAP and others), and it renders them deterministically to MP4 via headless Chrome and FFmpeg. It is designed to be driven by AI coding agents such as Claude Code. It suits exactly the motion-graphics format described (text, charts, UI, product images), and has no native live footage or AI-people generation.

### Cited Findings
- Official repo: heygen-com/hyperframes, maintained by HeyGen and "used in production at HeyGen". Tagline: "Write HTML. Render video. Built for agents." Licence Apache 2.0, with no per-render fees or commercial-use thresholds. Requires Node.js 22+ and FFmpeg. About 87.0k GitHub stars when fetched on 19 Nov 2026 (earlier snapshots showed 33.7k and 45.8k). — [GitHub heygen-com/hyperframes](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes); [awesome.ecosyste.ms](https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/github.com%2Fheygen-com%2Fhyperframes); [bestofjs](https://bestofjs.org/projects/hyperframes)
- Rendering seeks each frame in headless Chrome (Puppeteer) and encodes with FFmpeg. Rendering is deterministic: the same input gives the same frames. CLI: `npx hyperframes init my-video`, then preview and render. — [The Menon Lab](https://themenonlab.blog/blog/heygen-hyperframes-video-as-code); [silenceper (May 2026)](https://silenceper.com/en/article/2026-05-02-hyperframes-html-video-rendering/)
- Animation adapters: GSAP (paused timelines registered on `window.__timelines` for frame-accurate seeking), CSS, Lottie, Three.js, Anime.js, WAAPI. Audio tracks via data attributes. Captions and overlays. About 450 registry blocks (transitions, titles, charts, maps, 3D). Studio browser editor ("available, evolving"). Some skills require HyperFrames Pro. The README concedes Remotion has a more mature cloud renderer. Stated use cases: product launches, PR walkthroughs, data visualisations, captioned social videos, docs/PDF-to-video explainers, reusable motion graphics for automated pipelines. — [GitHub](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes); [skills.sh GSAP skill](https://www.skills.sh/heygen-com/hyperframes/gsap); [HyperFrames launch videos](https://hyperframes.heygen.com/launch-videos)
- Released in 2026 (DEV post; silenceper article dated 2 May 2026). Many unofficial mirrors and forks exist; use the heygen-com URL. — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/_46ea277e677b888e0cd13/heygen-hyperframes-an-open-source-video-framework-built-for-ai-agents-not-humans-mj8)

### Inferences
- Production cost is effectively €0 (open source, local render), so the €500 budget can go entirely to a product, tools, a domain or small paid tests.
- The ease of batch generation is double-edged. It enables high posting volume (Buffer: more outlier chances), but invites the "template with minimal differences" pattern that YouTube names as inauthentic. Vary substance, not just numbers.
- Being HeyGen's project, it sits beside HeyGen's commercial AI-avatar products. The reader's format excludes avatars, which avoids AI-people labelling issues.

### Gaps
- Exact first release date and current version number were not confirmed (the README did not state them; third-party snapshots listed v0.4.41 to v0.8.22).

2. Description of the service

FortyFive is a mobile navigation and route-planning application tailored to speed pedelecs (fast electric bicycles, class L1e-B / S-pedelec, with pedal assistance up to 45 km/h) in Flanders. The app calculates and visualises routes based on OpenStreetMap data, traffic-sign data and proprietary routing logic, and offers among other things:

  • Route planning and route visualisation
  • Turn-by-turn navigation with visual and spoken (TTS) turn instructions, including when the screen is locked
  • Local ride recording and ride history
  • Export of planned routes as a GPX file
  • Keeping your own reports on the map and displaying useful places (POIs such as bicycle parking and charging stations)
